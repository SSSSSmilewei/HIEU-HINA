## 5E. 部分分式积分法

**5E-2** $\displaystyle\int \frac{x\,dx}{(x - 2)(x + 3)}$

<details>
<summary>解答</summary>

用遮盖法：

$$
\frac{x}{(x - 2)(x + 3)} = \frac{2/5}{x - 2} + \frac{3/5}{x + 3},
$$

$$
\int \frac{x\,dx}{(x - 2)(x + 3)} = \frac25\ln|x - 2| + \frac35\ln|x + 3| + c.
$$

</details>

**5E-3** $\displaystyle\int \frac{x\,dx}{(x^2 - 4)(x + 3)}$

<details>
<summary>解答</summary>

用遮盖法：

$$
\frac{x}{(x - 2)(x + 2)(x + 3)} = \frac{1/10}{x - 2} + \frac{1/2}{x + 2} + \frac{-3/5}{x + 3},
$$

$$
\int \frac{x\,dx}{(x^2 - 4)(x + 3)} = \frac{1}{10}\ln|x - 2| + \frac12\ln|x + 2| - \frac35\ln|x + 3| + c.
$$

</details>

**5E-5** $\displaystyle\int \frac{(3x + 2)\,dx}{x(x + 1)^2}$

<details>
<summary>解答</summary>

$$
\frac{3x + 2}{x(x + 1)^2} = \frac2x + \frac{B}{x + 1} + \frac{1}{(x + 1)^2} \quad (\text{遮盖法});
$$

要求 $B$，比如代入 $x = 1$：

$$
\frac54 = 2 + \frac B2 + \frac14 \;\Longrightarrow\; B = -2.
$$

$$
\int \frac{3x + 2}{x(x + 1)^2}\,dx = 2\ln|x| - 2\ln|x + 1| - \frac{1}{x + 1} + c.
$$

</details>

**5E-6** $\displaystyle\int \frac{(2x - 9)\,dx}{(x^2 + 9)(x + 2)}$

<details>
<summary>解答</summary>

$$
\frac{2x - 9}{(x^2 + 9)(x + 2)} = \frac{Ax + B}{x^2 + 9} + \frac{C}{x + 2}.
$$

用遮盖法，$C = -1$。要求 $B$ 和 $A$：

$$
x = 0 \;\Longrightarrow\; \frac{-9}{9 \cdot 2} = \frac B9 - \frac12 \;\Longrightarrow\; B = 0,
$$

$$
x = 1 \;\Longrightarrow\; \frac{-7}{10 \cdot 3} = \frac{A}{10} - \frac13 \;\Longrightarrow\; A = 1.
$$

$$
\int \frac{2x - 9}{(x^2 + 9)(x + 2)}\,dx = \frac12\ln(x^2 + 9) - \ln|x + 2| + c.
$$

</details>

**5E-10h** $\displaystyle\int \frac{(x^2 + 1)\,dx}{x^2 + 2x + 2}$（要配方。）

<details>
<summary>解答</summary>

先做除法，再令 $y = x + 1$（于是 $x^2 + 2x + 2 = y^2 + 1$，$2x + 1 = 2y - 1$）：

$$
\begin{aligned}
\int \frac{(x^2 + 1)\,dx}{x^2 + 2x + 2} &= \int \left(1 - \frac{1 + 2x}{x^2 + 2x + 2}\right)dx = x - \int \frac{(2y - 1)\,dy}{y^2 + 1} \\
&= x - \ln(y^2 + 1) + \tan^{-1}y + c \\
&= x - \ln(x^2 + 2x + 2) + \tan^{-1}(x + 1) + c.
\end{aligned}
$$

</details>

## 5F. 分部积分；递推公式

**5F-1a** $\displaystyle\int x^a\ln x\,dx$（$a \ne -1$）

<details>
<summary>解答</summary>

$$
\int x^a\ln x\,dx = \int \ln x\,d\left(\frac{x^{a+1}}{a + 1}\right) = \ln x \cdot \frac{x^{a+1}}{a + 1} - \int \frac{x^{a+1}}{a + 1} \cdot \frac1x\,dx = \frac{x^{a+1}\ln x}{a + 1} - \int \frac{x^a}{a + 1}\,dx = \frac{x^{a+1}\ln x}{a + 1} - \frac{x^{a+1}}{(a + 1)^2} + c.
$$

（这里 $\int u\,dv$ 的写法就是分部积分：$u = \ln x$，$v = \frac{x^{a+1}}{a + 1}$。）

</details>

**5F-2d** 推导用 $\displaystyle\int x^{n-1}e^{ax}\,dx$ 表示 $\displaystyle\int x^ne^{ax}\,dx$ 的递推公式。

<details>
<summary>解答</summary>

$$
\int x^ne^{ax}\,dx = \int x^n\,d\left(\frac{e^{ax}}{a}\right) = \frac{e^{ax}}{a} \cdot x^n - \int \frac{e^{ax}}{a} \cdot nx^{n-1}\,dx = \frac{e^{ax}}{a}x^n - \frac na\int x^{n-1}e^{ax}\,dx.
$$

</details>

**5F-2b** $\displaystyle\int x^2e^x\,dx$

<details>
<summary>解答</summary>

$$
\int x^2e^x\,dx = \int x^2\,d(e^x) = x^2e^x - \int e^x \cdot 2x\,dx = x^2e^x - 2\int xe^x\,dx = x^2e^x - 2xe^x + 2e^x + c.
$$

（最后一步用到 5F-2a：$\displaystyle\int xe^x\,dx = xe^x - e^x + c$。）

</details>

**5F-3** $\displaystyle\int \sin^{-1}(4x)\,dx$

<details>
<summary>解答</summary>

$$
\begin{aligned}
\int \sin^{-1}(4x)\,dx &= x\sin^{-1}(4x) - \int x\,d\bigl(\sin^{-1}(4x)\bigr) = x\sin^{-1}(4x) - \int x \cdot \frac{4\,dx}{\sqrt{1 - (4x)^2}} \\
&= x\sin^{-1}(4x) + \int \frac{du}{8\sqrt u} \qquad (\text{令 } u = 1 - 16x^2,\ du = -32x\,dx) \\
&= x\sin^{-1}(4x) + \frac14\sqrt u + c \\
&= x\sin^{-1}(4x) + \frac14\sqrt{1 - 16x^2} + c.
\end{aligned}
$$

</details>
